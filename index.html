Current project

Dissertation research

Understanding Newcomer Immigrant Parental Engagement within U.S. Schools

A qualitative case study exploring newcomer parents’ engagement experiences, the role of social capital, and how educational institutions support or constrain engagement. The study centers families’ perspectives and attends to relationships among schools, families, and community organizations.

About

Research rooted in community.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Education Policy and Planning at The University of Texas at Austin. My scholarship examines educational equity through the experiences and knowledge of immigrant families.

I draw on community-engaged research and asset-based perspectives to explore how families participate in education—and how schools can respond more equitably.
